Chandubhai Virani

Founder & Managing Director of Balaji Wafers

Chandubhai Virani's story is one of India's most inspiring rags-to-riches entrepreneurial journeys. Born into an agricultural family in Gujarat, he and his brothers moved to Rajkot after facing severe financial difficulties in farming. With only 1,000 rupees provided by their father, they initially tried their hand at a farm equipment business, which unfortunately failed, leaving them struggling to make ends meet.

Refusing to give up, Chandubhai took up a job at the Astron Cinema in Rajkot, earning a meager salary. He eventually secured the contract for the cinema's canteen. Noticing the high demand for potato wafers among moviegoers and frustrated by the unreliable local suppliers, he decided to start making his own wafers in a small shed in his courtyard. His homemade wafers were a massive hit for their taste and quality.

What started as a small micro-business making wafers by hand in 1974 slowly transformed into a fully automated industrial operation by 1989. In 1992, "Balaji Wafers Private Limited" was officially incorporated. Chandubhai focused relentlessly on quality, affordability, and deep distribution networks, especially in rural and semi-urban areas.

Today, Balaji Wafers is a multi-million dollar empire and one of India's largest snack food brands, giving fierce competition to multinational giants like PepsiCo (Lay's). Despite his massive success, Chandubhai remains known for his humility, grounding his massive empire in the same values of hard work and customer satisfaction he learned in the cinema canteen.
